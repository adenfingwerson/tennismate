What to Expect at Your First Lesson

Your first lesson can cover the basics: grip, stance, forehand, backhand, footwork, and simple rallying. If you have played before, the session can focus on whatever feels weakest. If you have never picked up a racket, that is completely fine.

Because the session is private, you do not have to keep up with a class or worry about being the only beginner. The lesson is built around you.
