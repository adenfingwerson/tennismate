"But I'm Just a Recreational Player" — That's Exactly Who This Is For

One of the most common things I hear from players in Holdrege and Hastings is some version of: I'm not good enough for private lessons. That thinking has it completely backwards.

Recreational players benefit more from personalized instruction, not less. When you're just starting out or returning after years away from the game, small bad habits calcify fast. A forehand grip that's slightly off, a serve motion that puts strain on your shoulder, footwork patterns that are holding back your court coverage — these things are genuinely hard to self-diagnose, and they compound over time.

A good private lesson catches those issues early and replaces them with solid fundamentals. It's much easier to build correct habits from the start than to untrain years of muscle memory later.
