# Tennis T-Mate Monetization Roadmap

To convert our `r/tennis` and `r/10s` national traffic into direct revenue, we will execute the following pipeline.

## Phase 1: Traffic Foundation (Current)
- Generate high-value, culture-driven posts ("Sinner Mechanics", "Dead Strings", "Nebraska Wind").
- Distribute manually/automatically to Reddit and X to drive thousands of monthly impressions.

## Phase 2: Affiliate Revenue (Next 30 Days)
- Apply for the **Tennis Warehouse Affiliate Program** and **Amazon Associates**.
- We've added an `affiliate-box` CSS class to the blog templates. 
- *Action*: Update the "Poly vs Synthetic" string article with actual tracked affiliate links for Wilson NXT and RPM Blast.
- *Payout*: ~5-8% commission on any strings, racquets, or shoes purchased through the links.

## Phase 3: Digital Products (Next 60 Days)
- **The Product**: "The 6 UTR Playbook: Surviving Pushers & Heavy Wind"
- **Format**: A $15 PDF or unlisted YouTube video series linked via Gumroad/Stripe.
- **Integration**: At the bottom of tactics articles, offer the digital product instead of just local lessons for out-of-state visitors.

## Phase 4: Display Ad Networks (Milestone: 10k Visits/Month)
- **Target**: Ezoic or Mediavine.
- **Why**: Traditional Google AdSense pays pennies for sports traffic. Premium networks like Ezoic require a minimum baseline of traffic (~10k visits) but pay significantly higher RPMs (Revenue Per Mille) for high-intent sports/gear readers.
- **Implementation**: We will embed their script tags into the Cloudflare Pages `<head>` once we cross the traffic threshold.
