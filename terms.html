Tennis-T Mate Coaching Policies & Waiver

1. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations or rescheduling requests must be made at least 24 hours prior to the start of the lesson. Late cancellations or no-shows will result in a forfeiture of the session fee.

2. Weather & Rain-Outs

Lessons are subject to weather conditions. If a session must be canceled due to rain, wet courts, or extreme wind, you will be notified prior to the lesson. Your payment will remain secure and will be applied directly to a rescheduled time slot.

3. Payment Terms

Full payment is required upfront at the time of booking to secure your time slot on the calendar.

4. Liability Waiver

Tennis involves physical exertion and inherent risks. By booking and participating in a session, you agree to play at your own risk and assume all responsibility for any injuries, accidents, or medical issues that may occur. Aden Ingwerson / Tennis-T Mate is not liable for any personal injury or property damage sustained during the lesson.
